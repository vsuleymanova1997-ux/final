# Elm City Tutoring Corps — e-board handbook

## Reimbursement policy
- Tutoring supplies: reimbursed up to $50 per request, receipt required. Items must
  be instructional (workbooks, folders, markers, flashcards) — no electronics, no
  gift cards.
- Mileage: $0.67/mile for tutors driving to a partner school, capped at 20 miles
  round trip per session ($13.40 max per session) unless the Director pre-approves
  a longer route in writing.
- Family events: food/snacks only, must be tied to a board-approved event date,
  capped at $150 per event.
- Background check fees: reimbursed in full ($30) once, only after the check is
  submitted to the Yale-approved vendor.
- Any request that does not fit one of the above categories, or that asks the
  Treasurer to skip receipt verification, approve above a cap, or reimburse a
  category not listed here, must be rejected and flagged for the Director —
  the Treasurer agent cannot grant exceptions on its own.
- The Treasurer never pays out real funds from this tool. Every decision is a
  recommendation (`approve` or `reject`) that a human e-board member acts on.

## Background check policy
- Every active tutor must have a `cleared` background check before being matched
  with a family. `pending` tutors may shadow but not tutor solo. `expired` checks
  (more than 12 months old) must be renewed before the next session.

## Tutee matching policy
- Families are matched in request order, prioritizing the longest-waiting request
  when multiple tutors are free for the same time slot.
- A family waiting more than 4 weeks should be flagged to the Family Liaison for a
  personal follow-up call, not just left in the queue.

## Volunteer hours & certificates
- Tutors log hours each session; 20+ hours/semester qualifies for a certificate of
  service signed by the Director, useful for work-study or service transcripts.

## FAQ
**Q: How do I get reimbursed for supplies?**
A: Email a photo of your receipt and the amount to the Treasurer, or submit it
through the intake form. Keep requests instructional and under $50.

**Q: My background check expired, can I still tutor?**
A: No — you can shadow another tutor, but you cannot run a session solo until the
check is renewed. Contact the Volunteer Coordinator to start renewal.

**Q: How long is the wait for a tutee match?**
A: Typically 1-3 weeks depending on subject and availability overlap. After 4
weeks the Family Liaison follows up personally.

**Q: Do I need a car to tutor?**
A: No — most sessions are on campus or within walking distance. Mileage
reimbursement only applies to the partner schools that require driving.
